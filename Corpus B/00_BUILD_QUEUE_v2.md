## 1. What changed in v2

**Ordering** now follows the study schedule rather than emergency-first. Anything already built is marked and mapped to the week it serves.

**Every cluster is now split into parts (a, b, c…)**, one file per part, so no content is compressed out for space. Part **(a)** carries the framework and the must-not-miss material; **(b)** and **(c)** carry the depth, the long-tail differentials, and the examination and communication content that OSCEs test. That ordering is deliberate — see §2.

**Five categories in the schedule were not in `bl.md`** and have been added as new clusters: Geriatrics (expanded from 2 items to a proper block), Anaesthetics & Perioperative Care, Australian Context / Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Health, Public Health / Epidemiology / Statistics / Research, and EBM & Clinical Process. A Communication & Consultation Skills cluster has also been added for the OSCE. **These were not in the coverage report, so check the existing 150-file corpus before building — some may already exist.**

## 2. The arithmetic, stated plainly

**~193 parts remain.** At the observed 15–25 min per exchange that is **roughly 48–80 hours.**

Weeks 1–4 (everything before MCQ1 on 27 Sept) is **~146 parts, or 37–61 hours, across 4 weeks** — about 9–15 hours a week against roughly 3 hours a week of dedicated study time. The schedule as written does not fit, and no amount of reordering makes it fit.

**What the part-splitting is designed to do about that:** part (a) of every cluster is self-sufficient for the MCQ. If a week runs short, the (a) files across all of that week's topics are worth more than complete coverage of the first two topics and nothing on the rest.

**Recommended sequence: build the (a) layer across each week first, then return for (b) and (c) in October before the OSCE on 1 Nov.** That loses no OSCE content — it sequences it after the MCQ instead of before. The (a)-only route through Weeks 1–4 is about 62 parts, or 16–26 hours, which is close to achievable.

If you'd rather build strictly in order — a, b, c on each cluster before moving to the next — say so and I'll walk it straight down.
