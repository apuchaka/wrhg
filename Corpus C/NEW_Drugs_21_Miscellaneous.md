## 0.1 Specialised Drugs

> [!info] **What this subsection is.** AMH's final section is a residual category for agents that do not belong to a body-system class — **enzyme replacement therapies, agents for rare metabolic and genetic disease, diagnostic agents, radiopharmaceuticals, chelators for specific conditions, and other highly specialised products.** They are **initiated and monitored in designated centres, are often supplied through the Life Saving Drugs Program or similar arrangements, and an intern will not prescribe them.** What an intern needs is to **recognise that a patient is on one, find out what it is for, and not interrupt it.**

- **Enzyme replacement and substrate reduction therapies** — for **lysosomal storage disorders**: **agalsidase** (Fabry disease), **imiglucerase and miglustat** (Gaucher disease), **laronidase and related agents** (mucopolysaccharidoses), **alglucosidase alfa** (Pompe disease). Given as regular, often fortnightly, infusions; **infusion reactions and antibody formation** are the main issues, and **treatment is lifelong**. See [[15_17b_Paeds_-_Glycogen_Storage_Disorders__PKU__Lysosomal_Storage_Diseases]].
- **Agents for inherited metabolic disease** — **sodium benzoate and sodium phenylbutyrate** (nitrogen scavengers in urea cycle disorders), **carglumic acid**, **sapropterin** (BH4-responsive phenylketonuria), **nitisinone** (tyrosinaemia type 1), **carnitine**, and specialised metabolic formulas. **In an acute metabolic decompensation the priorities are stopping protein intake, giving intravenous dextrose to suppress catabolism, and calling the metabolic service — before the diagnosis is confirmed.** See `NEW_Investigations_General_and_Preventive.md` 0.6.
- **Disease-modifying therapies for rare neuromuscular and genetic disease** — **nusinersen, risdiplam and onasemnogene abeparvovec** (spinal muscular atrophy), **CFTR modulators** (see `NEW_Drugs_18_Respiratory.md` 0.4), **gene and cell therapies**. These have changed the natural history of previously fatal conditions, and **newborn screening for SMA in several Australian jurisdictions exists precisely because early treatment works** (see `NEW_Investigations_Orthopaedics_Neurology_and_Other.md` 0.17).
- **Diagnostic and contrast agents** — **iodinated contrast** (contrast-associated acute kidney injury, and the practical points about metformin and about pre-existing renal impairment); **gadolinium** (avoided in severe renal impairment because of **nephrogenic systemic fibrosis**, with the newer macrocyclic agents carrying much lower risk); **radiopharmaceuticals** (see `NEW_Investigations_Orthopaedics_Neurology_and_Other.md` 0.5 and `NEW_Investigations_General_and_Preventive.md` 0.9); and **stimulation and suppression testing agents** (synacthen, glucagon, insulin tolerance testing).
- **Miscellaneous agents an intern may meet:** **dantrolene** (malignant hyperthermia), **glucarpidase** (methotrexate toxicity), **sugammadex** (see `NEW_Drugs_02_Anaesthetics.md` 0.3.3), **hydroxocobalamin** (cyanide), **intravenous lipid emulsion** (local anaesthetic toxicity), and **defibrotide** (hepatic veno-occlusive disease).

> [!danger] **THE PRACTICAL RULES THAT APPLY TO ALL OF THESE, AND THEY ARE THE REASON THIS ENTRY EXISTS**
> 1. **NEVER OMIT, INTERRUPT OR SUBSTITUTE A SPECIALISED DRUG WITHOUT CONTACTING THE TREATING CENTRE.** These patients are frequently admitted under general teams for unrelated problems, and a missed infusion or a stopped enzyme replacement can undo years of treatment or precipitate acute decompensation.
> 2. **FIND OUT WHAT IT IS FOR.** A drug you have never heard of is not a reason to leave it off the chart — it is a reason to look it up and ring someone.
> 3. **CHECK INTERACTIONS.** Many of these agents have narrow indications and significant interactions, and the treating centre knows them.
> 4. **THE PATIENT AND THEIR FAMILY ARE USUALLY THE EXPERTS.** In rare disease, they know their condition, their regimen and their emergency plan better than the admitting team does. **Ask them, and read the emergency management plan they carry** — many patients with metabolic disease carry one, and it is written precisely for the situation you are in.
> 5. **DOCUMENT THE TREATING SERVICE AND A CONTACT NUMBER** in the admission notes and the discharge summary.
