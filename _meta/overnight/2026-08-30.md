### Finding — adrenaline for anaphylaxis has THREE locations, not two

`PENDING_GUIDELINE_CHECKS.md` **B71** records two owners. Measured, there are three:

| File | Figure |
|---|---|
| `09_01_Dermatology_-_Dermatological_Emergencies` | `0.01 mg/kg` up to a maximum of `0.5 mg` |
| `NEW_Drugs_01_Allergy_and_Anaphylaxis` | `0.01 mL/kg`, max `0.5 mL (0.5 mg)`, injector bands **from 7.5 kg** |
| `15_01b_Paeds_-_Anaphylaxis` | ASCIA verified box |

**The two that state a figure express the same dose in different units** — mg in one, mL in
the other. That is exactly how two owners drift without either looking wrong, and it is worse
than a plain duplicate because a unit conversion sits between them. **B71 should be updated
from two owners to three.** Not edited here — `PENDING_GUIDELINE_CHECKS.md` is
append-never-delete and manual.

### Dismissed, with reason

**Cardiac-arrest adrenaline is correctly separate.** `01_Cardiovascular` `1 mg` (adult) and
`15_01a_Paeds` `10 mcg/kg` are a *different indication* from anaphylaxis. Consolidating them
with the ASCIA table would be the error, not the fix. Recorded in `OWNERS.md` so a later
session does not "tidy" them together.

### Detector refined mid-run — first version had ~30% false positives

The first pass returned **338**. Sampling 18 showed roughly a third were **not verification
claims at all**: `Gap-filled from CSV…`, `Why this file exists`, `Sourcing limitation applying
to this whole file`, `Standalone build, not yet integrated`, `Build status`, `Scope error`.
Those record provenance or build state, not a check against a source.

**That is above the ~20% halt threshold, so the detector was rebuilt rather than used.**
Adding an exclusion list dropped it to **290** — 48 excluded.

**Residual false-positive rate in P1 is roughly 20% by sample**, and the survivors are
warnings or corrections rather than verification claims (*"This entry treats hypoglycaemia
without ever defining it"*). Those are marked **`[?]`** in the proposals file. The rate is
acceptable here **only because nothing is written**: every line is a draft for a human, and a
false positive costs a discarded proposal rather than a false assurance in a file.

## Follow-ups · ✅

### The 11 MIXED markers — why the original sample saw only 4

**The sample was drawn from what MOVED, not from what was wrong.** I measured MIXED by
diffing classifications before and after the source-list extension — so only markers that
*changed category* appeared. **6 of the 11 already scored `OPEN` before the extension**, never
moved, and were therefore invisible to a diff-based sample.

| Prior label | n | Visible in the diff? |
|---|---|---|
| was `LOGIN` | 5 | yes — these are the ones reported |
| was `OPEN` | 6 | **no — nothing moved, so nothing showed** |

The 7 not in the original sample: `A2_Airway_Compromise` ("all doses from **ASCIA and eTG**"),
`A4_Dyspnoea`, `A6_Thermal`, `C1_Acute_Abdomen` (Australian Immunisation Handbook + eTG),
`08_09_Infectious_Disease`, and two further markers inside `F0-4` beyond the one counted.

**The lesson is about the sampling frame, not the count:** a before/after diff can only show
you what changed. Markers that were *already* mislabelled were stable, and stability read as
correctness.

### 🛑 A defect found in my own MIXED work — recorded, NOT fixed

`actionability()` is evaluated over **the marker scope plus the entire line**, so an unrelated
source name elsewhere on the line leaks into the classification.

`08_09` L17 is a **verification box** that mentions **RCH**, and happens to contain an
`UNVERIFIED` marker whose scope is *"AU regimen; Therapeutic Guidelines (login)"*. The RCH
belongs to the box's own claim, not to the marker — but the classifier sees both and returns
`MIXED`. On the scope alone it is `LOGIN`.

**Measured impact: 2 markers of 360.** And the two point opposite ways:

| | line-based | scope-based | which is right |
|---|---|---|---|
| `08_09` L17 | MIXED | LOGIN | **scope** — RCH is the box's, not the marker's |
| `A6` L290 | OPEN | UNKNOWN | arguable — the line names a source that may genuinely settle it |

There is a principled case for scope-only (§1.7 requires every marker to name its own source,
so `A6`'s marker failing to do so *should* triage). **I did not make the change.** It alters
queue semantics for a real if small set, the two cases disagree, and this is not a decision to
take unilaterally overnight. **Left for a decision — see WHAT I NEED FROM YOU.**
