## Step 28d — `_meta/OWNERS.md` · ✅

**Examined:** every line in all 240 corpus files matching `RE_DOSE`, cross-matched against a
43-drug name list, grouped by drug with its file, line, `population:` and section.

**Result:** 43 drugs carry at least one dose-bearing line. **31 of them state figures in more
than one file**; 12 have a single unambiguous owner. **24 entries carry `RANGE NOT STATED`** —
a figure given without saying who it applies to, which is the B65 shape and the actionable
finding in this file.

### Finding — adrenaline for anaphylaxis has THREE locations, not two

`PENDING_GUIDELINE_CHECKS.md` **B71** records two owners. Measured, there are three:

| File | Figure |
|---|---|
| `09_01_Dermatology_-_Dermatological_Emergencies` | `0.01 mg/kg` up to a maximum of `0.5 mg` |
| `NEW_Drugs_01_Allergy_and_Anaphylaxis` | `0.01 mL/kg`, max `0.5 mL (0.5 mg)`, injector bands **from 7.5 kg** |
| `15_01b_Paeds_-_Anaphylaxis` | ASCIA verified box |

**The two that state a figure express the same dose in different units** — mg in one, mL in
the other. That is exactly how two owners drift without either looking wrong, and it is worse
than a plain duplicate because a unit conversion sits between them. **B71 should be updated
from two owners to three.** Not edited here — `PENDING_GUIDELINE_CHECKS.md` is
append-never-delete and manual.

### Dismissed, with reason

**Cardiac-arrest adrenaline is correctly separate.** `01_Cardiovascular` `1 mg` (adult) and
`15_01a_Paeds` `10 mcg/kg` are a *different indication* from anaphylaxis. Consolidating them
with the ASCIA table would be the error, not the fix. Recorded in `OWNERS.md` so a later
session does not "tidy" them together.
