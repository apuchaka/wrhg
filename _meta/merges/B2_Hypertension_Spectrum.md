## A note on phaeochromocytoma

It has **no disease entry anywhere in the vault** — `grep "^#+.*haeochromocytoma"` returns
nothing, and it appears only inside the MEN2A/MEN2B lists at `06_Metabolic` §0.9.

**It is nonetheless not recorded as a gap**, because all three parts a reader needs are
present in other files: the presentation at `NEW_Cardiology_and_Vascular` §Paroxysmal
Hypertension, the screening test at `NEW_Investigations_Renal_and_Urology` §0.10, and the
alpha-before-beta safety rule at `NEW_Drug_Classes_Cardiovascular_Antihypertensives:86`.
**Recorded here so the absence is visible rather than merely unmentioned**, and so a later
round can decide whether the scattering is acceptable. Building a section for it is out of
scope for a B-file merge.
